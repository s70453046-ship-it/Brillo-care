Ask about your space ↗
How do I request a quote?

Complete the quote form with your contact details, property type, location and service. It prepares an email draft for you to review and send using your own email application.

What affects the price of a cleaning service?

The quote may depend on property size, current condition, service type, frequency, access arrangements and specific tasks requested.

Do you offer one-off and recurring cleaning?

You can request a one-off or recurring service. Available arrangements can be discussed when your request is reviewed.

Do you clean both homes and businesses?

BrilloCare is presented for residential properties, holiday rentals, offices and commercial spaces in Malta.

Do I need to be present during the clean?

That depends on the property and access arrangements. Mention your preference so arrangements can be discussed beforehand.

How should I prepare the property?

If possible, put away valuable or fragile items, secure pets, and make sure the areas to be cleaned are accessible.

What does end-of-tenancy cleaning include?

The scope can vary with the property and handover requirements. Describe the size, condition, inspection date and areas needing particular attention.

What information is needed after construction work?

Tell us the type of renovation, approximate property size, current site condition, whether work is finished, and any remaining dust or debris.
